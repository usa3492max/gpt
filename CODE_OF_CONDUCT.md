# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a security project, so "decent" also has a specific meaning: do not use this
tool — or this community — to harm systems or people who did not agree to be tested.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith and argue about code, not people.
- Keep criticism specific: what broke, where, and what you expected instead.
- Respect the scope of the project. It is a pentest agent for **authorised** work; requests to make
  it stealthier, harder to detect, or usable against systems without consent are out of scope and
  will be declined.
- Respect disclosure norms. Do not post another party's live vulnerability, credentials, customer
  data, or flags from platforms whose rules forbid sharing them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Asking for, or providing, help to attack systems without authorisation.
- Sharing malware, weaponised exploits against unpatched third-party software, or stolen data.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions, and any space where you are
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via the repository's **Security →
Advisories → Report a vulnerability** form, or by opening an issue asking for a private channel
without including sensitive details. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban
contributors whose behaviour violates this document.
